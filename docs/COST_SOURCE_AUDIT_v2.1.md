Important: shallow suction handpumps cannot substitute for Taradev/deep water-table equipment without engineering assessment. The prior submersible pump reference is retained as an optional **unverified** motorized-well item and excluded from handpump repair estimates. Technician labour (Tk 1,800/day), emergency tanker delivery (Tk 6,500/trip), repair parts (Tk 4,000/set), pipes and other placeholders remain **scenario assumptions**. No defensible district-specific service quote was found. Obtain written quotations from DPHE/NGOs or suppliers before procurement. Water safety requires independent verification after repairs.

The worksheet transfers only user-selected Repair and Transport category costs to the planner after explicit confirmation. `Reference only` rows are not transferred. All values are editable. The app does not scrape or auto-update prices.
